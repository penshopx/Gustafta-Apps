---
name: LMS certificate permanence
description: Product rule for retaining issued Academy certificates when their source course is deleted.
---

Issued LMS certificates are permanent records. Deleting a course, including a force-delete, must preserve every certificate already issued and keep its public verification code working from a course-title snapshot.

**Why:** A learner's earned credential should not become invalid merely because an administrator cleans up or retires the source course.

**How to apply:** Course deletion may remove lessons, enrollments, and progress, but it must detach rather than delete issued certificates. Any certificate display or verification flow must support a missing source course.
